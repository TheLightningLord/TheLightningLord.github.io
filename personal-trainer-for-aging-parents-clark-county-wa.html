How It Works

Gentle From the Very First Minute

A relaxed first call — family welcome

Common Worries

"But My Parent Is…" — I Hear These a Lot

🕰️

"…too old or too frail"

A landmark study saw frail nursing-home residents averaging around 90 make real strength gains in weeks. Frailty is a reason to start carefully — not a reason to skip it.

🦯

"…uses a cane or walker"

Perfect candidate. We train seated, at the counter, and with support — building the exact strength that makes an aid feel less necessary over time.

🙅

"…resistant to the idea"

Very common. I lead with dignity and patience, start absurdly small, and let the wins do the convincing. No drill-sergeant energy — ever.

🌎

"…and I live out of town"

You can set everything up from anywhere and stay updated remotely. I can also coach them live online if that fits better.

Questions Families Ask

Straight Answers

Can I hire you for my parent even if they're hesitant?+

Yes, and it's one of the most common ways families start. I keep the first session gentle and pressure-free, meet your parent with respect, and let small early wins build their buy-in. You're welcome to be there.

Where in Clark County do you work with seniors?+

In their home, anywhere in Clark County — Vancouver, Battle Ground, Camas, Washougal, Ridgefield, Salmon Creek, Hazel Dell, and the surrounding communities.

My parent has arthritis / a replacement / a heart condition. Is training safe?+

These are reasons to train smart, not to avoid it. Doctoral-level PT education means the program is built around their conditions, and I coordinate with their healthcare providers when appropriate. This is educational fitness coaching, not medical treatment.

How much does it cost, and how often would they train?+

Senior focus sessions are $45 for 30 minutes, typically 1–5× per week depending on their goals, with discounts on packs of 8 or more. The first consultation is free. Full details on the pricing page.

Can we start with the family and not just my parent alone?+

Absolutely — I encourage it. Family involvement makes older adults feel safer and more consistent. Join the first call, be in the room, and stay as involved as you'd like.
