Questions

Clark County FAQ

What areas of Clark County do you cover?+

All of it — Vancouver, Battle Ground, Camas, Washougal, Ridgefield, La Center, Brush Prairie, Hockinson, Salmon Creek, Hazel Dell, Orchards, Yacolt and the surrounding communities. If you're near the county line, just ask when you book and I'll let you know.

Do I need any equipment or a home gym?+

No. I bring the equipment your program needs. All you provide is a small space to move — a living room, garage, patio, or a spot in a nearby park works perfectly.

How much does in-home personal training cost?+

In-home sessions are $75 for a 60-minute 1-on-1 and $45 for a 30-minute senior focus session, with discounted rates on packs of 8+ sessions. Full details are on the pricing page, and your first consultation is always free.

Do you train seniors and people recovering from injury?+

Yes — those are two of my specialties. Senior strength and fall prevention and post-physical-therapy training are exactly what my clinical education is built for. See the Senior Resource Center and the Bridge program.

What if I travel or split time between homes?+

Every program is also available live online, so your training follows you anywhere in the world — in your own language, thanks to real-time translation. Learn about online coaching →
