Why It Matters

The Physiology of Thriving

Understanding the clinical benefits of geriatric strength training.

🧠

Neuroplasticity

Complex movement patterns stimulate the creation of new neural pathways, protecting against cognitive decline and dementia.

🦴

Bone Density

Wolff's Law states that bones adapt to loads. Our resistance protocols actively combat osteopenia and osteoporosis.

⚖️

Fall Prevention

By strengthening the fast-twitch fibers in the lower legs and core, we dramatically reduce the risk of catastrophic falls.

🩸

Metabolic Health

Muscle is the largest endocrine organ. Increasing mass improves insulin sensitivity and regulates blood glucose.

😊

Mood Elevation

Exercise releases myokines and endorphins that naturally combat depression and anxiety common in isolation.

🏠

Independence

The ultimate goal. Maintaining the strength to perform Activities of Daily Living (ADLs) without assistance.
