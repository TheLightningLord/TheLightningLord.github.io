Concierge Care FAQ

Transparency and clinical clarity for our clients and their families.

Does this replace my existing PT?
We can work in tandem with traditional PT or as a step-down program once you've been discharged. We focus on long-term athletic maintenance for seniors, bridging the gap between "medical necessity" and "elite vitality."
What geographical areas do you serve?
We serve Battle Ground, Vancouver, and the greater Clark County area. We bring the entire clinic to your private residence, assisted living facility, or memory care center.
How are family members kept in the loop?
We provide an optional secure digital dashboard where designated family members can view session summaries, biometric trends, and clinical progress reports.
