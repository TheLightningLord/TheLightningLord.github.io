The Science Behind What We Do

Exercise is one of the most powerful “medicines” we have — especially for older adults. Our programming is aligned with major guidelines and research in cardiology, endocrinology, neurology, and gerontology, translated into realistic, senior-friendly plans.

Heart & Blood Pressure

Regular, moderate movement helps lower blood pressure and improves cholesterol patterns, reducing your risk of heart disease and stroke when practiced consistently over time.

Blood Sugar & Metabolism

Simple post-meal walks and gentle resistance exercises help your muscles soak up blood sugar, supporting better A1c and long-term metabolic health in people with or at risk for type 2 diabetes.

Brain & Mood

Movement increases blood flow and growth factors like BDNF in the brain, supporting memory, attention, and mood. Exercise is strongly linked with lower risk of cognitive decline and depression over time.

Balance, Falls & Bones

Strength, power, and balance training together can reduce fall risk, support bone density, and make everyday tasks like getting out of a chair or stepping onto a curb feel more secure.
