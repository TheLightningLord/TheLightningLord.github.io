The Core Disciplines of Longevity

Founded by Zeuse Valentine, Olympian Health Solutions bridges the gap between exercise science and active aging. Our specialized approach to senior health is rooted in three key pillars.

Applied Kinesiology

Utilizing functional human mechanics to improve range of motion and decrease joint pain. Our protocols are designed for safe, impactful movement in a home environment.

Geriatric Strength Training

Evidence-based resistance training tailored specifically for older adults to prevent sarcopenia, improve bone density, and enhance daily independence.

Metabolic Nutrition

Fueling for longevity through personalized nutritional insights that support metabolic health, cognitive function, and sustained energy levels throughout the day.

Common Insights

Expert answers to your longevity questions.

Why is Kinesiology important for seniors? +
Kinesiology ensures that every movement is biomechanically sound. For seniors, this means reducing the risk of injury while maximizing the benefits of exercise for joint mobility, balance, and pain reduction. Zeuse Valentine applies these scientific principles to every session.
Do you provide equipment for home visits? +
Yes. Olympian Health Solutions is a fully mobile fitness service. We bring professional-grade equipment, including stability tools, resistance bands, and weights, directly to your home in Clark County.
Can strength training help with Osteoporosis? +
Yes. Resistance training is clinically proven to stimulate bone growth and improve density. Our geriatric strength training protocols are specifically designed to safely combat Osteoporosis and Sarcopenia (muscle loss).
