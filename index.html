The Physiology of Rejuvenation

Evidence-based mechanisms of action. See how targeted movement reverses specific biological markers of aging.

Sarcopenia Reversal
Muscular Hypertrophy

113% increase in muscle strength in 90-year-olds after 8 weeks of high-intensity training.

Source
Fiatarone et al. JAMA, 1990.
Cellular Aging
Telomere Preservation

Highly active adults have significantly longer telomeres, accounting for 9 years of reduced biological aging.

Source
Tucker. Prev Med, 2017.
Energy Production
Mitochondrial Density

Resistance training reverses the age-related decline in mitochondrial oxidative capacity in skeletal muscle.

Source
Melov et al. PLoS ONE, 2007.
Systemic Health
Inflammatory Markers

Regular moderate exercise lowers basal levels of CRP and IL-6, key markers of chronic systemic inflammation.

Source
Petersen & Pedersen. J Appl Physiol, 2005.
Neuroplasticity
Hippocampal Volume

Aerobic exercise increases the size of the anterior hippocampus (memory center) by 2%, reversing age-related loss.

Source
Erickson et al. PNAS, 2011.
Metabolic Health
Insulin Sensitivity

Skeletal muscle contraction increases glucose uptake via GLUT4 translocation, independent of insulin action.

Source
Richter & Hargreaves. Physiol Rev, 2013.
Osteoporosis
Bone Mineral Density

Site-specific resistance training is proven to maintain or increase bone density in post-menopausal women.

Source
Zhao et al. Sports Med, 2014.
Safety
Fall Reduction

Multi-component exercise interventions reduce the rate of falls by 23% and the number of people falling by 15%.

Source
Sherrington. Cochrane Database, 2019.
