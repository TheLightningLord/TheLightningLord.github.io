    Why Exercise Works Better Than You Think

    Exercise affects almost every system in the body. Here’s what the research consistently shows for adults 55+.

    Better Daily Energy

    • Improves oxygen delivery to your muscles and brain.
    • Helps regulate sleep cycles and daytime alertness.
    • Reduces chronic fatigue linked to inactivity.

    Stronger Joints & Less Pain

    • Strengthens muscles that support painful joints.
    • Improves mobility and reduces stiffness.
    • Helps the nervous system feel safe moving again.

    Greater Independence

    • Improves balance and reduces fall risk.
    • Makes stairs, walking, and daily tasks easier.
    • Helps maintain the freedom to live how you want.

    These benefits come from small, consistent amounts of movement—often far less than people expect. Your program will always match your body, your health, and your starting point.
