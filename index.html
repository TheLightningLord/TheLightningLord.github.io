Meet Your Coach

Zeuse Valentine

Founder & Coach, Olympian Health Solutions · Battle Ground, WA

📍 Battle Ground, WA 🌿 Senior Fitness 🛡️ Post-PT & Injury 🔬 Science-based

I didn't start strong. At 90 pounds, I was the scrawny kid who picked up a barbell hoping to change — and over the next few years I added about 70 pounds of muscle. Exercise didn't just reshape my body. It genuinely saved my life.

Then I tore up my shoulder — badly. For a while I couldn't raise my arm overhead or even turn my neck without severe pain, and losing the ability to train sent me into a deep depression. Physical therapy at ProActive here in Battle Ground brought me back, and it lit a fire: I fell in love with sports medicine and dedicated my life to helping people recover and rebuild.

I grew up with my grandparents, and today I live with and care for my grandpa — so I know firsthand what strength, independence, and confidence mean as we age. That's why senior fitness and post-injury training are my specialties. Every program I write is grounded in my B.S. in Kinesiology from Oregon State and doctoral physical therapy education — never guesswork.

Questions

Before You Ask the Oracle

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?+

Absolutely — beginners often see the fastest progress. Every program starts with an assessment and is built around your current level. You'll never be thrown into something you're not ready for.

What makes you different from other trainers?+

Education and individualization. I hold a B.S. in Kinesiology/Exercise Science from Oregon State and am currently in Hawai‘i Pacific University's Doctor of Physical Therapy program. Your program is built on movement science, not recycled templates.

I have an old injury or chronic pain. Can you help?+

This is my specialty area. While personal training isn't a substitute for medical care, my doctoral PT education means I program intelligently around limitations and coordinate with your healthcare providers when needed.

I'm over 60 — am I too old to start?+

Not even close. Working with older adults is one of my specialties. Strength and balance training is among the best-proven ways to protect your independence, prevent falls, and stay capable for the things you love — and every program is scaled patiently to where you are today.

How does in-home / portable training work?+

I come to you — your living room, garage, yard, or a nearby park — and bring the equipment and the plan. No gym membership, no commute, no intimidation. We just need a small space to move. It's training built around your life, not the other way around.

How fast will I see results?+

Most clients feel a difference in 2–3 weeks and see visible change by week 6–8. Consistency beats intensity — and my job is engineering a plan you can actually stick to.

What if I'm not satisfied?+

If you complete your first month of coaching, follow the plan, and don't feel it was worth it — I'll work with you until it is. Your results are my reputation.

What areas does Olympian Health Solutions serve?+

Portable, in-home and on-site personal training across Clark County, WA — based in Battle Ground, where there's no travel fee. I bring everything to you.
