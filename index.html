Meet Your Coach

You deserve a coach who's
been there too.

Zeuse Valentine · Founder & Coach, Olympian Health Solutions

I know what it's like to lose your strength — and your confidence in your own body. I've been there. And I know the way back.

At 90 pounds, I was the scrawny kid who picked up a barbell hoping to change — and added about 70 pounds of muscle over the years that followed. Then a serious shoulder injury took it all away. Physical therapy at ProActive in Battle Ground brought me back and lit a lifelong passion for sports medicine and recovery.

Today I'm the primary caregiver and coach for my 87-year-old grandfather — so I understand training around real bodies, real fears, and real life. That's why senior fitness and post-injury training are my specialties, and why every program is grounded in clinical science, never guesswork.

"Exercise saved my life. Now my mission is simple — to help as many people as I can find that same strength." — Zeuse Valentine

Questions

Before You Ask the Oracle

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?+

Absolutely — beginners often see the fastest progress. Every program starts with an assessment and is built around your current level. You'll never be thrown into something you're not ready for.

What makes you different from other trainers?+

Education, individualization, and real empathy. I'm a NASM Certified Personal Trainer with a B.S. in Exercise Science from Oregon State, currently in a Doctor of Physical Therapy program — and I'm the caregiver and coach for my own 87-year-old grandfather. Your program is built on movement science and genuine understanding, not recycled templates.

I have an old injury or chronic pain. Can you help?+

This is my specialty area. While personal training isn't a substitute for medical care, my doctoral PT education means I program intelligently around limitations and coordinate with your healthcare providers when needed.

I'm over 60 — am I too old to start?+

Not even close. Working with older adults is one of my specialties. Strength and balance training is among the best-proven ways to protect your independence, prevent falls, and stay capable for the things you love — and every program is scaled patiently to where you are today.

How does in-home / portable training work?+

I come to you — your living room, garage, yard, or a nearby park — and bring the equipment and the plan. No gym membership, no commute, no intimidation. We just need a small space to move. It's training built around your life, not the other way around.

How fast will I see results?+

Most clients feel a difference in 2–3 weeks and see visible change by week 6–8. Consistency beats intensity — and my job is engineering a plan you can actually stick to.

What if I'm not satisfied?+

If you complete your first month of coaching, follow the plan, and don't feel it was worth it — I'll work with you until it is. Your results are my reputation.

I don't speak English fluently — can we still train together?+

Absolutely, and it works beautifully. Real-time translation runs through our live sessions, so you hear my coaching in your language and I understand you in yours — cues, questions, encouragement, all of it. Coaching has reached people in 50+ languages; yours is almost certainly one of them.

I live outside the U.S. — can you coach me?+

Yes. Online coaching is fully global — we train live over video from anywhere in the world, scheduled around your time zone. The free FitPack ships within the U.S.; if you're international, I'll send a simple gear list so you can grab the equivalent locally (it's inexpensive and easy to find), and we go from there.

How does the free FitPack shipping work?+

Every online coaching plan includes a FitPack — a complete, drawer-sized home gym — shipped to your door free anywhere in the U.S. It arrives within days, sets up in five minutes, and you can train the same day. No equipment to shop for, no membership, no commute.

What areas does Olympian Health Solutions serve?+

Online coaching is available worldwide — train live from anywhere, in your language. For in-person training, I offer portable, in-home sessions across Clark County, WA, based in Battle Ground, where there's no travel fee. I bring everything to you.
