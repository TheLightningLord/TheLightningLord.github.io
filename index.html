The Science of Aging Backwards

Exercise isn't just "good for you." It triggers specific, measurable physiological changes that reverse aging markers. Here is the data.

Sarcopenia Reversal
113%

Increase in muscle strength observed in 90-year-old nursing home residents after just 8 weeks of high-intensity resistance training.

i
Fiatarone et al. JAMA, 1990.
Fall Prevention
-23%

Reduction in the rate of falls among older adults who participated in exercise programs compared to controls.

i
Sherrington et al. Cochrane Database Syst Rev, 2019.
Cognitive Health
+2%

Increase in hippocampal volume (memory center) after one year of aerobic exercise, effectively reversing age-related loss by 1-2 years.

i
Erickson et al. PNAS, 2011.
Bone Density
Stop Loss

Resistance training is proven to halt the age-related decline in bone mineral density and can increase density by 1-3%.

i
Zhao et al. Sports Medicine, 2014.
