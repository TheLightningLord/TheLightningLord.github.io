The Clinical Efficacy Dashboard

We prescribe specific movement dosages to achieve measurable biological outcomes. Click a condition to see the data.

Type 2 Diabetes ▼
Mechanism: GLUT4 Translocation

Muscle contraction moves glucose from the blood into cells without needing insulin. This "invisible insulin" effect lasts for up to 48 hours after a session.

Risk -42%
Source: Sigal et al., Diabetes Care (2006)
Knee Osteoarthritis ▼
Mechanism: Synovial Fluid Dynamics

Movement pumps nutrient-rich synovial fluid into the joint capsule, lubricating the cartilage. This mechanical action is more effective than painkillers for long-term management.

Pain -40%
Source: Moseng et al., BJSM (2017)
Fall Risk ▼
Mechanism: Type II Fiber Recruitment

Falls happen when you can't move your feet fast enough to catch yourself. Power training targets fast-twitch fibers to improve reaction time and stability.

Risk -34%
Source: Sherrington et al., Cochrane (2019)
Cognitive Decline ▼
Mechanism: BDNF Release

Aerobic exercise triggers Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor, which acts like fertilizer for new neurons in the hippocampus (memory center).

Volume +2% (Reverses Shrinkage)
Source: Erickson et al., PNAS (2011)
