Questions

Battle Ground FAQ

Do you actually come to my home in Battle Ground?+

Yes. In-home, portable training is the whole idea. I come to your house, garage, yard, or a nearby park anywhere in Battle Ground and the surrounding area — Meadow Glen, Brush Prairie, Hockinson, and out toward Battle Ground Lake.

How much does a personal trainer cost in Battle Ground?+

In-home sessions are $75 for a 60-minute 1-on-1 and $45 for a 30-minute senior focus session, with discounts on packs of 8 or more. Your first consultation is free. See the pricing page for full details.

I'm a total beginner — is that okay?+

More than okay. Beginners often make the fastest progress. Every program starts with an assessment and is scaled to exactly where you are today.

Do you train seniors in Battle Ground?+

Yes — senior strength and fall prevention is a specialty, and in-home training is ideal for older adults. See the Senior Resource Center or the page on training for aging parents.

What if I'd rather train online?+

That's available too. Live online coaching means your training follows you anywhere — great for travel or busy weeks. Learn more →
