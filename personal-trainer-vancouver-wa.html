Vancouver FAQ

Which parts of Vancouver do you cover?+

All of it — Salmon Creek, Hazel Dell, Felida, Cascade Park, Fisher's Landing, Uptown, downtown near Esther Short, Orchards, and the surrounding neighborhoods. Farther out in the county? See all of Clark County.

How much does a personal trainer cost in Vancouver?+

In-home sessions are $75 for a 60-minute 1-on-1 and $45 for a 30-minute senior focus session, with discounts on packs of 8 or more. Your first consultation is free. See the pricing page.

Do I need equipment or a home gym?+

No. I bring the equipment your program needs. You just provide a small space to move — indoors or out.

Do you train seniors in Vancouver?+

Yes — senior strength and fall prevention is a specialty, and in-home training is ideal for older adults. See the Senior Resource Center or the page on training for aging parents.

Can I train online instead?+

Absolutely. Live online coaching keeps your training going through travel and busy weeks. Learn more →
